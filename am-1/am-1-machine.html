The Machine

Three arpeggio parts share one clock at divisions /1 to /4. A drone plays under them. Every note comes from the current chord, and the chord comes from the selected key and scale. RUN starts and stops the clock.

Harmony

KEY and SCALE set the pitch set. Six scales are available, plus free, which is chromatic and does not snap. The DEGREE buttons move the whole ensemble to another chord in the key while it plays. 7THS adds a seventh to every chord. Chords can also be latched from the keyboard. All input is snapped to the scale.

Parts

The three parts are BASS, RYTM and LEAD. Each part has a voice (saw, square, glass, bell, pluck), a pattern (up, down, updown, random, seq, rev), a clock division, an octave span, note SHAPE, and LEVEL. LEN sets loop length from 0 to 16. At 0 the pattern uses its natural length. Parts with unequal lengths drift out of phase and meet again. Hold 1, 2 or 3 to double a part’s rate.

Sequencer

SEQ REC arms recording. In melody mode the keyboard enters up to 16 notes, and REST enters a gap. A part set to seq plays the recording. rev plays it backward. FOLLOW moves the recording with the degree buttons. In chords mode the degree buttons record a progression instead, and REST records a hold. While running, a recorded progression advances the harmony one step per whole note. Melody and progression run at the same time. Steps in the bar can be clicked on and off. The moving marks show where each part is.

Drone

Five voices: saw, square, sine (one octave up), strings, and choir. Strings is a saw pair through an ensemble circuit. Choir is a fixed vowel with slow vibrato. NOTE puts the drone on the root, the fifth, or a high octave. The drone follows KEY, PITCH, LFO 2 and ENTROPY.

Effects

DELAY is a tempo-synced ping-pong. Repeats lose treble as they go. REVERB has four rooms: hall, plate, spring, dark. PHASER is a four-stage sweep. The left and right channels sweep a quarter cycle apart.

Mod

Two LFOs. LFO 1 reaches audio rate and targets cutoff, shape, drive, reverb or resonance. LFO 2 is slower and targets pitch, feedback, delay, level or phaser. Waves: tri, saw, square, random, chaos. Chaos follows a Lorenz attractor and never repeats. At rate 0 a cycle takes about thirty minutes.

Master

TEMPO runs 5 to 180. SWING shifts every second pulse. PITCH transposes up to an octave either way without touching the clock. GLIDE slides between notes. DRIVE is clean transistor color at low settings, clipping in the upper range, and aliased at the top. ENTROPY loosens playback from 0 to 100. At 0 the machine is exact. At 100, pitch wanders past a semitone, notes scoop in late, and about one in five plays weak. Timing is not affected. FEEDBACK returns the output through a delay tuned to the key. Above about 90 it howls on the tonic. CUTOFF and RESONANCE are one filter over everything. OUTPUT is the volume.

Era

The year selects the circuit. 2026 has exact oscillators and symmetric drive. 1983 has pulse-width oscillators, free-run phase, and asymmetric drive with bias memory. In 1983 the display also takes on a CRT filter. Each patch stores its year.

Anomaly

Rolls a complete patch inside the current scale.

Patches

38 factory patches. Patches save by name and travel as JSON files through export and import. The session is stored automatically and comes back on the next visit, year included. INIT clears the machine to zero. BOUNCE records the output to a WAV file.

Keys

space run/stop · T tap tempo · 1/2/3 hold to ratchet · REST during recording.

Texture

Stratified ostinato polyphony over a pedal point: interlocking arpeggios over a drone.
