AM-1. Three arpeggio layers share one clock at divisions /1 /2 /3 /4 — they interlock by construction. Every note is drawn from the latched chord, and the chord is drawn from the scale: the machine cannot leave the key. Scale free drops the rails — all twelve notes, no snapping. Change degrees while it runs; that is the performance. SEQ REC arms the keyboard — tap a melody (REST for gaps), and any layer set to seq loops it. FOLLOW transposes the sequence with the degrees — the Berlin move. Delay is tempo-synced — the classic river. Reverb is the hall it runs through. MOD is one slow wave aimed at one thing — the hands-free sweep. Bounce records the output as WAV. Space runs; T taps tempo. ANOMALY rolls a legal patch — the rails hold. FEEDBACK returns the machine to itself; past ninety it howls. No pages. No wrong notes.
