The dots are the tape — the only thing recorded. Each dot casts a rippled bump (its value × the sinc shape), and the bright line is simply all bumps summed: the one band-limited curve the samples define. The read head takes its value off that line. The recipe follows where you read; the tape supplies what is there — they meet at sixteen multiplies and never mix.
