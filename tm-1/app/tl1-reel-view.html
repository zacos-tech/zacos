The reels are the readout. Hub diameter is how much tape is left — supply shrinks, take-up grows, and a full six-hour reel visibly transfers from one side to the other. Rotation is true angular velocity, so a nearly empty reel spins faster than a full one at the same tape speed. Set the two decks a few thousandths apart and the drift readout is the machine describing its own imperfection. Raise Spool × to watch hours pass.
